## 1. Brand positioning

> **"Boutique comfort in the heart of G-9."**

Velmore Inn is a compact boutique hotel with finishes you'd usually see in a much bigger property: travertine reception, a bronze feature wall with LED lettering, a floating timber staircase, a lift lobby with LED strips, coffered ceilings with warm cove lighting, and white-marble floors.
The social feed should make people think *"that's a lot nicer than I expected for an inn"*, and then make it easy to call.

**Who we're talking to**

| Audience | What they care about | Hook angle |
|---|---|---|
| Business travellers (Islamabad meetings, I&T Centre, Blue Area) | Location, quiet room, fast check-in | "Your meeting is 10 min away. Your bed is 10 steps away." |
| Families visiting patients at PIMS (next door in G-8) | Close, clean, twin/family rooms, safe | "Visiting PIMS? Stay minutes away." |
| Out-of-city families and shoppers (Karachi Company, G-9 Markaz) | Value, space, safety | "Two master beds. One room. PKR 11,000." |
| Couples and weekend visitors | Aesthetics, privacy | "A weekend that looks this good." |

## 3. Visual identity

### Colour palette (sampled from Velmore's own interiors)

| Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Espresso | `#2B1D14` | Main dark background, footer bar |
| Bronze Leather | `#8A5A2B` | Secondary panels (matches reception wall) |
| Velmore Gold | `#C9A04E` | Logo, headline accents, dividers, icons |
| LED Amber | `#F2B33D` | Small highlights only (CTA button, price tag) |
| Travertine | `#EDE3D1` | Light backgrounds, cream panels |
| Marble White | `#F7F4EE` | Text on dark backgrounds |

Rule: **at most 3 colours per post** (Espresso + Gold + one neutral).

### Typography (all free in Canva)

| Role | Font | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | **Cinzel** (matches the logo's Roman caps) | `BOUTIQUE COMFORT` |
| Elegant accent word | **Great Vibes** or **Allura** (script) | *Exquisite*, *Welcome* |
| Sub-headline | **Cormorant Garamond** Semi-bold | Experience premium comfort |
| Body / contact | **Montserrat** Medium | 0326 6125000 |

Headline 70–110 pt · script accent 90–140 pt · body 28–34 pt. Keep text to **12 words or fewer** on the image itself.

### Photography

- Use only real Velmore photos (reception, lift lobby, staircase, deluxe lounge room, family room, twin room). Keep stock photos to a minimum.
- Warm grade: +10 warmth, +5 contrast, slight vignette. Never cool/blue.
- Leave the top 20% or the bottom 25% of the frame calm for the headline.

## 5. Hook strategy

People decide in about **1.5 seconds** whether to stop scrolling. Every post opens with one of these 8 hook types, and we rotate them so the feed never repeats itself two days in a row.

| Hook type | Formula | Velmore example |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Curiosity / reveal** | "You won't believe this is…" | "This isn't a 5-star lobby. It's G-9." |
| **2. Location** | "[Landmark] is X minutes away" | "PIMS is minutes away. So is a good night's sleep." |
| **3. Pain → relief** | Name the problem, then show the fix | "Tired of hotels that look nothing like the photos?" |
| **4. Question** | Ask something they'll answer in their head | "Where are you staying in Islamabad this weekend?" |
| **5. Contrast / POV** | "Expectation vs reality" or "POV:" | "POV: you walked in expecting an inn." |
| **6. Social proof** | Quote a guest or a number | "'Cleanest room I've had in Islamabad.' (guest review)" |
| **7. Scarcity / FOMO** | Dates, limited rooms | "New Year's Eve: only a few rooms left." |
| **8. Detail close-up** | One material or craft detail | "Floating timber. Hidden LEDs. Every step is lit." |

### Caption structure (every post)

1. **Line 1: hook** (repeat or extend the on-image hook, ≤ 10 words)
2. **Lines 2–3: value** (what the guest gets)
3. **Line 4: CTA** → "📞 Call / WhatsApp 0326 6125000"
4. **Line 5: location** → "📍 Plot 13, Street 54, I&T Center, G-9/4, Islamabad"
5. **Hashtags (8–12):** mix of brand, local and niche

**Hashtag bank**
`#VelmoreInn #VelmoreInnIslamabad #G9Islamabad #IslamabadHotels #HotelInIslamabad #StayInIslamabad #BoutiqueHotelPakistan #IslamabadDiaries #FamilyStayIslamabad #BusinessTravelPakistan #PIMSIslamabad #VisitIslamabad`

### Posting rhythm

- **5 feed posts a week** (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat) → 20–22 posts a month
- Best slots for Pakistan audiences: **1:00–2:00 pm** and **8:30–10:30 pm** PKT
- Share every feed post to Stories with a "Call Now" link sticker
- Reply to every comment within 1 hour during the first 3 hours

## 6. Do / Don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| One hero photo per post | Collage more than 4 photos |
| Gold on Espresso, cream on photo | Neon colours, bright red/green |
| Real photos of Velmore | AI or stock rooms presented as Velmore |
| Phone + logo on **every** post | Post without contact details |
| Short hooks (≤ 12 words) | Paragraphs of text on the image |
| State only amenities we can confirm | Claim pool/breakfast/parking unless confirmed |
